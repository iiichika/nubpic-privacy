# NubPic Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

## 1. We collect no data

NubPic does not collect or upload any personal information, and no data is stored on the developer's servers. Photos are processed only on your device, and photos you explicitly import into the App Library are stored only on this device. The app contains no networking code, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no push SDKs. Importing, redacting, and exporting still work in Airplane Mode; photos that exist only in iCloud must be downloaded first.

## 2. How photos are handled

Photos you explicitly import into the App Library are stored in the app's on-device library solely to run detection, redaction, and export on your device. Photos imported from the system library keep a reference and a preview by default; the original stays in Photos and is read only when you open or export it, which requires Photo Library access. You can save the original for an individual photo so it no longer depends on the system library; a referenced photo may be unavailable if the original is deleted, exists only in iCloud, or access is revoked. Photos imported with the system picker or camera are stored as local copies. The library remains on this device until you delete it or uninstall the app, is excluded from iCloud backup, and is never uploaded. Directly opening a system photo for editing uses a temporary copy that is cleared when you switch away. The app can browse your library only after you explicitly allow access to the Photo Library. If you don't allow it, you can still import with the system picker, and the app sees only the photos you choose.

## 3. Permissions

- **Camera**: used only when you choose to take a photo that needs redaction.
- **Photo Library (read, optional)**: requested only when you open the Photo Library tab; used to browse and pick photos. If you decline, you can still import with the system picker.
- **Photo Library (add-only)**: used only to save the redacted copy back to your library.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in Settings; sharing still works after revocation.

## 4. Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs and never provides photos, recognized text, or device information to any third party.

## 5. Metadata

By default, exported photos have location, device model, capture time, and other metadata removed. You can turn off "Remove Metadata" on the export screen; if you do, the exported file may retain that information. The choice is entirely yours.

## 6. Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information from children, or from anyone else.

## 7. Your choices

You can delete individual photos or clear the App Library at any time inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. Originals in the Photos library are never modified.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page in the app and revise the effective date.
