# NubPic Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

## 1. We collect no data

NubPic does not collect, upload, or store any personal information. The app contains no networking code, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no push SDKs. Every feature works in Airplane Mode.

## 2. How photos are handled

Photos you select are copied into the app's sandboxed temporary directory solely to run detection, redaction, and export on your device. That directory is cleared when the app exits or restarts. We cannot access any other photo in your library.

## 3. Permissions

- **Camera**: used only when you choose to take a photo that needs redaction.
- **Photo Library (add-only)**: used only to save the redacted copy back to your library. The app never requests read access to your library.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in Settings; sharing still works after revocation.

## 4. Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs and never provides photos, recognized text, or device information to any third party.

## 5. Metadata

By default, exported photos have location, device model, capture time, and other metadata removed. You can turn off "Remove Metadata" on the export screen; if you do, the exported file may retain that information. The choice is entirely yours.

## 6. Children's privacy

The app does not collect any information from children, or from anyone else.

## 7. Your choices

You can clear imported photos at any time inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page in the app and revise the effective date.
